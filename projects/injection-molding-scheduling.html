Production Scheduling for Plastic Injection Molding

Research · UT Space Institute · IISE Annual Conference 2023

Problem

A plastics manufacturer needed to assign jobs to parallel injection-molding machines that differ in capability, while respecting shared resource limits.

What I did

  • Collaborated on a resource-constrained, unrelated parallel-machine scheduling formulation (mixed-integer linear program).
  • Solved it with Gurobi using data from the manufacturer.

Results

  • Presented at the IISE Annual Conference & Expo, New Orleans, May 2023 (with A. Owens and A. J. Yu).

Tools: MILP, Gurobi
