Learning-Based Optimization for the Traveling Salesman Problem

Research · UT Space Institute · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, 2024

28%shorter tours than a graph pointer-network baseline
59%less runtime on 1,000-node instances
IEEE T-ITSjournal article

Problem

Large traveling salesman problems are slow to solve exactly. This work trains a model to predict which parts of a high-quality tour are likely, then uses those predictions to guide solution methods.

What I did

  • Supervised learning predicts structural components of high-quality solutions.
  • The predictions are integrated with exact and heuristic solution methods.

Results

  • Published benchmarks showed 28% shorter tours with 59% less runtime than a graph pointer-network baseline on 1,000-node instances.
Collaborative work; I am the second author.

Tools: Supervised learning, combinatorial optimization
