Doctoral Research

Physician Scheduling Optimization

Problem
Build multi-site physician schedules for a federally funded, multi-institution project with Prisma Health that meet staffing rules while respecting rest, fatigue, fairness, and individual preferences.
Method
Workforce scheduling models (MILP and CP-SAT) in Python with Gurobi and Google OR-Tools, run on a high-performance computing cluster, plus a bi-objective model trading clinician fatigue against schedule quality and fairness over a 90-day horizon.
Result
Schedules met an average of 84–87% of each physician's weighted preference categories across synthetic 25–75-physician benchmarks while satisfying all hard staffing rules.
Winter Simulation Conference

Bullwhip Effect Simulation

Problem
Reduce demand-signal amplification (the bullwhip effect) across a multi-stage supply chain.
Method
Agent-based supply chain simulation in AnyLogic testing information sharing and risk pooling.
Result
Combining both reduced simulated order-to-demand variance by 46.3% at the factory and 49.3% at the wholesaler.
IEEE T-ITS Journal

Learning-Based Optimization for the TSP

Problem
Solve large traveling salesman problems quickly without losing tour quality.
Method
Co-authored a supervised-learning approach that predicts structural components of high-quality solutions and integrates them with exact and heuristic methods.
Result
Published benchmarks showed 28% shorter tours with 59% less runtime than a graph pointer-network baseline on 1,000-node instances.
Graduate Class Project

Bike-Sharing Demand Forecasting

Problem
Forecast hourly bike-rental demand in Seoul from weather, time-of-day, season, holiday, and operating-status features.
Method
Compared a neural network, a PyTorch LSTM on sliding-window sequences, XGBoost, and random forest; tuned with Ray Tune, staged grid search, and GridSearchCV.
Result
Raised the neural network's test R² from 0.91 to about 0.96 and identified demand drivers through feature-importance analysis.
Graduate Class Project

Emergency Department Admission Prediction

Problem
Predict which emergency department visits end in hospital admission.
Method
End-to-end Python workflow combining structured predictors with TF-IDF features from chief-complaint text; compared logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost, CatBoost, and LightGBM with cross-validated grid search.
Result
CatBoost reached the best ROC-AUC (0.863).
Graduate Class Project

Dynamic Physician Allocation with MDPs

Problem
Decide how to allocate physicians between critical and mild-patient queues over time.
Method
Discounted Markov decision process from a uniformized queueing model, solved by value iteration, policy iteration, modified policy iteration, and a Gurobi dual linear program.
Result
Compared the solution methods on convergence, value functions, and policy agreement.
Team Project · Fresenius Medical Care

Reducing Production Downtime

Problem
Low overall equipment effectiveness (OEE) on automated production lines.
Method
Analyzed four months of company-provided data, decomposing OEE into availability, performance, and quality and attributing breakdowns across failure modes.
Result
Proposed an integrated failure-reporting system, standardized data entry, a supporting database, and standard operating procedures.
