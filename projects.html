Research projects

  • Agent-Based Simulation of the Bullwhip Effect

    Research · UT Space Institute · Winter Simulation Conference 2022
    AnyLogic simulation showing how information sharing and risk pooling reduce order amplification in a multi-stage supply chain.

  • Learning-Based Optimization for the Traveling Salesman Problem

    Research · UT Space Institute · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, 2024
    Co-authored a supervised-learning approach that guides exact and heuristic solvers on large routing problems.

  • Deep Reinforcement Learning for Perishable Inventory

    Research · UT Space Institute · IISE Annual Conference 2023
    Reinforcement-learning ordering policies for perishable products with stochastic demand, deterioration, and quantity discounts.

  • Production Scheduling for Plastic Injection Molding

    Research · UT Space Institute · IISE Annual Conference 2023
    Mixed-integer model for scheduling jobs on parallel molding machines under resource limits, using a manufacturer's data.

Graduate and industry projects

  • Predicting Emergency Department Admissions

    Graduate class project · Applied Data Science, Clemson University, Spring 2025
    Compared five classifiers on 347,360 emergency department visits using structured data and chief-complaint text.

  • Bike-Sharing Demand Forecasting

    Graduate class project · team project with A. Hines
    Forecast hourly bike rentals in Seoul with neural networks, an LSTM, XGBoost, and random forest.

  • Dynamic Physician Allocation with Markov Decision Processes

    Graduate class project · Clemson University
    When should a physician switch between critical and mild-patient queues? Solved as a Markov decision process four ways.

  • Network Interdiction for Wildlife Trafficking

    Graduate class project · IE 8700, Clemson University, Fall 2023
    Which routes should enforcement block to make trafficking of poached tiger parts as hard as possible?

  • Facility Location with a Custom Branch-and-Bound

    Graduate class project · MATH 8120, Clemson University, Fall 2024
    Built a branch-and-bound solver from scratch and benchmarked it against an integer solver and the LP relaxation.

  • Lagrangian Relaxation with Subgradient Updates

    Graduate class project · team project
    Relaxed hard resource constraints and tuned a subgradient method to get bounds and feasible schedules.

  • Reducing Production Downtime at Fresenius Medical Care

    Team project with Fresenius Medical Care
    Analyzed equipment effectiveness on automated production lines and proposed a failure-reporting system.

  • Deep Q-Network Agent for the Board Game Ludo

    Team project · University of Tennessee, Knoxville
    Trained a reinforcement-learning agent to play Ludo and tested how design choices affect it.
