Selected projects

  • Agent-Based Simulation of the Bullwhip Effect

    Research · UT Space Institute · Winter Simulation Conference 2022
    AnyLogic simulation showing how information sharing and risk pooling reduce order amplification in a multi-stage supply chain.

  • Learning-Based Optimization for the Traveling Salesman Problem

    Research · UT Space Institute · IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, 2024
    Co-authored a supervised-learning approach that guides exact and heuristic solvers on large routing problems.

  • Predicting Emergency Department Admissions

    Graduate class project · Applied Data Science, Clemson University, Spring 2025
    Compared five classifiers on 347,360 emergency department visits using structured data and chief-complaint text.

  • Bike-Sharing Demand Forecasting

    Graduate class project · team project with A. Hines
    Forecast hourly bike rentals in Seoul with neural networks, an LSTM, XGBoost, and random forest.

  • Network Interdiction for Wildlife Trafficking

    Graduate class project · IE 8700, Clemson University, Fall 2023
    Which routes should enforcement block to make trafficking of poached tiger parts as hard as possible?
