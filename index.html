Published research · IEEE T-ITS

Learning-based routing optimization

Combining supervised learning with optimization. On 1,000-node instances, published benchmarks showed a 28% smaller average optimality gap and 59% less computation time than a graph pointer network.

Explore the work →

Published research · WSC

Supply-chain simulation

An agent-based model explores information sharing and risk pooling. Combining both reduced simulated order-to-demand variance by 46.3% at the factory and 49.3% at the wholesaler.
